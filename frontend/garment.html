Garment Studio

Give the measurement chart. The pattern pieces are drawn from it, cut larger by your fabric's own shrinkage, laid on a marker to find the fabric each garment takes, and made into a garment you can turn in 3D. Change a figure and everything follows.

1 Fabric

The chart is the finished, flat, after-wash garment. The shrinkage is how much this cloth shrinks in the wash: the pieces are cut that much larger. The wales and courses only draw the knit on the 3D garment.

2 Measurement chart

Flat widths, as a spec sheet gives them. * The chest and the length are needed; anything else left empty is taken from the chest, and the chart says so. The marker ratio is how many garments of each size are cut together.
