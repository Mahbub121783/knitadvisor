Fabric scan

Photograph a knitted swatch under the scan card. The loops are counted on this phone (no AI, nothing uploaded but the numbers); the knitting spec is worked out from the factory's own records.

Not connected to a screen — results show here.

1The card

Print the scan card at 100 %. Lay the fabric flat, face up, wales running top to bottom. Then either cut out the card's window and lay it on the fabric, or — no scissors — lay the card right beside the fabric: the scanner finds the fabric on any side.

Print the card (PDF)
  1. Phone straight above the card, 8–12 cm away; the four black squares fill most of the picture.
  2. Even light — no shadow of the phone, no flash glare.
  3. Take 2–3 photos at different places. A clip-on macro lens helps fine jersey (over 20 wales/cm).
  4. Then one photo of the back: it tells jersey from interlock and shows fleece loops.

2Face photos — 1 to 3

Each photo is measured as soon as it is taken.

The camera checks the distance, straightness, focus and light by itself and takes each photo when everything is right: 3 of the face, then the back.

3Back photo — optional

Turn the fabric over, same card. It helps tell the structure.
