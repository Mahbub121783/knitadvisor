Carbon footprint

The CO2e of one kilogram of finished fabric, cradle to mill gate, worked out from the process itself: the fibre and what is lost making it into yarn and cloth, the electricity of every machine, the heat to warm every dye bath, the water every dryer has to boil off, and the boiler and grid behind them. Every uncertain figure is a range, and the range is shown. Then the strongest ways to cut it, each one re-run through the same model.
