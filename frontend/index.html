04Method

Calibrated on real cloth, not on brochures.

The engine’s constants come from production records of a knitting floor — machine, yarn, stitch length and the finished width and weight that came off it.

2,201

Greige-to-finish records

Each pairs what was set on the machine with what the finished fabric measured. Structure constants, count curves and tightness bands are read from them.

±4%

Checked against the floor

Predicting each piqué record’s finished weight from the other records lands within 4% at the median, and the check is redone whenever the records change.

Labelled

Measured or modelled, said plainly

Every figure carries its source: factory data, a published relation, or a model. Where a value is an estimate, the page says so.
