WHY MILLS USE IT

One spec sheet, every answer.

Everything a knitting floor needs to cost, source and produce a fabric, solved together from a single input.

Yarn count & loop length

Ne, tex and denier with the full formula trace, plus loop length for any of 70 knit constructions.

Machine spec & production

Gauge, dial diameter, feeders and needles resolved together, then kg per hour, per shift and per day once RPM and efficiency are set.

Costing from real inputs

Fabric price per kg from live yarn prices and dyeing recipes, then garment CMT and FOB with consumption, allowances and cutting loss.

Quality & fibre advisory

Predicts shrinkage and spirality risk and flags composition and process mismatches before machine time is booked.

K/T/M pattern & 3D preview

Knit, tuck and miss laid out visually with the needle sequence behind it, and a 3D view of the construction.

Tech pack, RFQ & carbon

Export a fabric tech pack PDF, take buyer quote requests with reference pricing, and see a screening-level carbon footprint.
