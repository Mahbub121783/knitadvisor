Carbon footprint

Know the carbon in every kilo of cloth.

Choose a composition and see what the fibre and the mill energy add up to — computed by the same engine, from the same specification, in the same click.

  • Stage by stage: fibre, spinning, knitting or weaving, dyeing and finishing, each charged on the mass that actually passes through it.
  • Wet processing from physics: the heat to warm each bath, the water each dryer evaporates, the boiler and the grid behind them.
  • Per kg, per m² and per garment in the calculator, against a conventional-cotton baseline.
  • A range, not a false point. Every uncertain input carries low, central and high values, and the answer shows which one it hangs on. Your own meter data replaces the model where you have it.
