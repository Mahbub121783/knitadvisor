Yarn price desk

Where every yarn price in KnitAdvisor comes from, live from the system: which markets are read and whether they answered, how a quote that is weeks old is brought to today by what its fibre has done since, what changes in another country, and what duty, VAT, freight and LC charges add. The costing sheet and the fabric calculator use exactly these prices.

The price chain — every yarn price passes these steps, and the result says which ones it took

1Market quote

The Bangladesh yarn market's list of imported yarn against LC, in USD per kg, by product and count, with the date the market put on it.

2Between counts

A count the list does not print is read between the two nearest quotes of the same day, at most 8 Ne apart, and badged as read-between, never as a quote.

3Brought to today

An older quote moves by what its fibre has done since: Δ price = share × kg of fibre in a kg of yarn × Δ fibre price, from the cotton benchmark and, for polyester, crude oil.

4Country

Another country: the same fibre, spun on that country's published industrial power tariff. Energy is about 30% of the spinning cost.

5Buying basis

Export mill on a back-to-back LC through a bonded warehouse: no duty, no VAT. Otherwise freight, duty, LC charges and VAT, each its own line.

✓Price used

With its badge and date on every costing, so any figure can be traced back to the market and the day it came from.
